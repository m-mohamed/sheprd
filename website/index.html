Smart sessions for Herdr.

Find the project, choose the agent, and connect to the right Herdr workspace.

Herdr owns the runtime. sheprd owns the way in.

The missing layer.

Find

Discover repos from configured roots and match them to Herdr workspaces.

Connect

Create or focus the right workspace without memorizing live Herdr IDs.

Stay out

Herdr keeps owning sessions, panes, tabs, remotes, and agent state.

Sesh for Herdr.
